The importance of high-density bases in mattress longevity

A mattress is a substantial investment, and its lifespan is heavily dictated by what lies at the very bottom. Without a proper foundation, even the most premium comfort layers will begin to sag prematurely.

High-density bases provide the crucial structural integrity needed to support the pocket coil system above. They act as a shock absorber, diffusing pressure across the entire surface area of the bed and preventing localized wear and tear.
