Why pocket coils are the gold standard for couples who share a bed

Motion isolation isn't just about comfort; research shows disrupted sleep from a partner's movement can reduce REM duration by up to 30%. When you share a bed, every toss and turn has the potential to pull your partner out of deep sleep.

This is where individually wrapped pocket coils make a transformative difference. Unlike traditional innerspring systems where coils are wired together, pocket coils move independently. When weight is applied to one coil, the surrounding coils remain unaffected.
