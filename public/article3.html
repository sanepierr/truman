How mattress firmness affects spinal alignment through the night

Not all "medium-firm" is created equal. The industry standard for firmness often fails to account for the unique pressure points of individual bodies, leading to lower back pain and stiffness upon waking.

True spinal neutrality is achieved not just by surface firmness, but by the intelligent layering of materials beneath. A mattress must be soft enough to contour to your hips and shoulders, yet firm enough to prevent your midsection from sinking.

Truman's multi-layered construction features adaptive transition foams sitting above robust pocket coils, ensuring that your spine remains in its natural alignment regardless of your sleeping position.
