The link between sleep temperature and deep sleep quality

Core body temperature naturally drops during sleep onset. This subtle change in thermodynamics signals your brain that it is time to transition into the deeper stages of sleep, where physical and mental restoration occurs.

If your sleep environment traps body heat, your core temperature rises, triggering micro-awakenings. This is the primary reason for waking up feeling exhausted despite getting a full eight hours of sleep.

Our gel-infused memory foam accelerates heat dissipation. By pulling warmth away from your body, it actively supports your body's natural cooling process, helping you achieve deeper, longer, and higher-quality sleep.
